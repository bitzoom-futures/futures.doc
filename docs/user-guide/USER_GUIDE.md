### Place an Order

Configure your trade using the order form on the right side of the trading screen.

1. **Choose Margin Type** &mdash; Select between Cross and Isolated margin
2. **Set Leverage** &mdash; Tap the leverage button (e.g., **20x**) to adjust
3. **Select Order Type** &mdash; Choose **Limit** or **Market**
4. **Enter Price** &mdash; For Limit orders, set your price. Tap **BBO** for the best available price
5. **Enter Amount** &mdash; Specify the quantity in base asset or USDT
6. **TP/SL** &mdash; Set Take Profit and Stop Loss levels
7. **Time in Force** &mdash; Choose **GTC**, **FOK**, or **IOC**
8. Tap **Buy/Long** (green) to go long, or **Sell/Short** (red) to go short

### Withdraw Funds

To withdraw cryptocurrency from your Bitzoom account:

1. Go to the **Account** tab and tap **Send**
2. **Select a coin** from the list
3. Enter the **destination wallet address**
4. Choose the **network** (chain) for the transfer
5. Enter the **withdrawal amount**
6. Review the **network fee** and confirm
